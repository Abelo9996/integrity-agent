# Responsible use

This is an evidence-gathering screening aid for qualified research integrity reviewers.
An anomaly is not evidence of intent and is not a verdict about a researcher.

- Do not publish outputs about individuals or use the tool for public accusations.
- Send concerns privately to the relevant journal or research integrity office.
- Expert human review of the original evidence and plausible benign explanations is required.
- Check attribution, declared reuse, common experiments, preprint versions and corrections.
- Respect licenses, privacy, API terms, access controls and robots.txt. Never bypass a paywall.
- Keep downloaded papers, cache entries, manifests and reports in ignored local folders.
- Obtain permission before sending paper content to a model provider; LLM processing is opt-in.
- A negative screen does not establish reliability. A positive screen does not establish misconduct.

Anomalies are not proof of misconduct. Findings require expert human review.
